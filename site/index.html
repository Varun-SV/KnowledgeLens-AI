01 / THE DIFFERENCE

AI answers are easy.
Inspectable reasoning is harder.

KnowledgeLens keeps the graph between you and the model. Every useful relationship can carry its own source, page or chunk, evidence, and confidence—so the answer is not the end of the trail.

02 / TRACE A THOUGHT

Watch unstructured material become navigable knowledge.

INGEST 01

Bring the messy stuff.

PDFs, Markdown, text, JSON, XML, and source code become source-aware chunks instead of one anonymous text blob.

spec.pdfapi.pynotes.md
EXTRACT 02

Turn prose into claims.

The model extracts structured subject → relationship → object claims while carrying evidence and provenance forward.

{ "subject": "Cache", "relation": "reduces", "object": "Latency" }
CONNECT 03

Let relationships accumulate.

Claims from different sources coexist instead of being flattened into one generic edge. Agreement, tension, and cross-document links become visible.

TRACE 04

Follow the evidence backward.

Hover a relationship to inspect exactly which source produced it and where the supporting evidence lives.

ASK 05

Ask through the graph.

KnowledgeLens retrieves relevant entities, neighboring claims, and short graph paths before the LLM answers—rather than throwing the whole document pile at it.

03 / PROOF, NOT FOG

The edge is not the evidence.

Many knowledge-graph demos can tell you that two things are connected. KnowledgeLens is moving toward something stricter: each claim remains its own inspectable object.
